Area Guide

From Yokohama's illuminated waterfront to the serene shores of Kamakura, Hayama, Shonan and Zushi — discover the neighborhoods that define exceptional living in Kanagawa.

Yokohama

Yokohama

Cosmopolitan, connected and framed by one of Japan's most recognizable waterfronts, Yokohama offers an exceptional balance of urban energy and residential ease. Minatomirai appeals to those seeking contemporary towers and concierge living, while Yamate and Motomachi offer leafy streets, international heritage and proximity to established schools. Direct rail links place central Tokyo within practical commuting distance.

📊 Average Rent: ¥180,000 – ¥650,000/month (2LDK–4LDK)

Highlights

  • ✦ 30 min to Tokyo Station via JR Tokaido/Yokosuka Line
  • ✦ Yokohama International School, Saint Maur International nearby
  • ✦ World-class dining, Chinatown, Red Brick Warehouse
  • ✦ Established international community with bilingual services
  • ✦ Major hospitals with English-speaking staff
  • ✦ Waterfront parks, marina, cultural venues

Kamakura

Kamakura

Kamakura combines historic temples, wooded hills and a relaxed coastal rhythm less than an hour from central Tokyo. Residences range from restored timber homes in Kita-Kamakura to modern villas near Yuigahama and Shichirigahama. The area is especially prized by creative professionals and families seeking nature, culture and a strong sense of community.

📊 Average Rent: ¥150,000 – ¥500,000/month (2LDK–4LDK)

Highlights

  • ✦ 55 min to Tokyo via JR Yokosuka Line
  • ✦ UNESCO-caliber cultural heritage and shrines
  • ✦ Scenic beaches: Yuigahama, Shichirigahama, Zaimokuza
  • ✦ Hiking trails connecting temples and nature
  • ✦ Active community of artists, writers and entrepreneurs
  • ✦ Farm-to-table dining and artisan cafés

Hayama

Hayama

Hayama is a discreet seaside enclave known for sheltered beaches, sailing culture and spectacular sunsets across Sagami Bay. Spacious villas, private gardens and ocean-view estates define its upper market. Its unhurried atmosphere attracts executives, entrepreneurs and families seeking privacy without losing access to Yokohama and Tokyo.

📊 Average Rent: ¥200,000 – ¥700,000/month (2LDK–5LDK)

Highlights

  • ✦ Imperial Villa grounds and pristine Isshiki Beach
  • ✦ Renowned sailing and yachting community
  • ✦ Spectacular sunset views across Sagami Bay to Mt. Fuji
  • ✦ Private villas and estate-sized properties available
  • ✦ Quiet, refined atmosphere favored by executives
  • ✦ Access to Yokohama (40 min) and Tokyo (70 min)

Shonan

Shonan

Stretching along the coast around Fujisawa, Tsujido and Enoshima, Shonan offers an energetic blend of beach culture and modern convenience. New residential developments, excellent shopping and dependable rail connections make it attractive for both year-round living and weekend escapes. On clear days, many coastal homes enjoy unforgettable views of Mount Fuji.

📊 Average Rent: ¥120,000 – ¥400,000/month (2LDK–3LDK)

Highlights

  • ✦ 50 min to Shinjuku via Odakyu Line / JR Shonan-Shinjuku
  • ✦ Iconic Enoshima island and world-class surfing
  • ✦ Mount Fuji views from coastal properties
  • ✦ Modern malls: Terrace Mall Shonan, Shonan T-SITE
  • ✦ Family-friendly parks, cycling paths, outdoor lifestyle
  • ✦ Growing coworking and remote-work community

Zushi

Zushi

Zushi combines a welcoming international community with a compact town center, sandy beach and easy access to both Kamakura and Hayama. Hillside neighborhoods such as Hiroyama offer privacy and panoramic sea views, while Kotsubo provides marina-side living. Direct rail services to Yokohama and Tokyo add everyday practicality to its resort-like character.

📊 Average Rent: ¥140,000 – ¥550,000/month (2LDK–5LDK)

Highlights

  • ✦ Direct JR Yokosuka Line to Yokohama (25 min) and Tokyo (55 min)
  • ✦ Welcoming, established international community
  • ✦ Hiroyama hillside
