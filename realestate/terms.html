Privacy Policy

Kanagawa Premier Realty (the "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal data in connection with our website and services.

2.1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: full name, email address, telephone number, postal address and nationality
  • Inquiry Details: inquiry type, property preferences, budget, preferred areas and messages submitted via our contact form
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, time spent on pages and other analytical data collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies
  • Transaction-Related Information: documentation and identity verification data provided during property transactions, where applicable

2.2 How We Use Your Information

We use collected personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • To recommend suitable properties based on stated preferences
  • To facilitate property viewings, negotiations and transactions
  • To send newsletters, market updates and promotional materials (with consent)
  • To improve our website, services and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations, including those under the Building Lots and Buildings Transaction Business Act

2.3 Cookies and Local Storage

The Site uses cookies and browser local storage to enhance your browsing experience. This includes remembering language preferences, maintaining your favorites list and gathering anonymous usage statistics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit certain features of the Site.

2.4 Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With property owners, landlords or their agents as necessary to facilitate a specific transaction you have requested
  • With professional service providers (judicial scriveners, tax accountants, immigration professionals) when coordinating services on your behalf, with your knowledge
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process or government request
  • With analytics providers for anonymized and aggregated data analysis

2.5 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

2.6 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes. Transaction-related records are retained in accordance with the record-keeping requirements of the Building Lots and Buildings Transaction Business Act and other applicable regulations.

2.7 Your Rights

Under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction, addition or deletion of inaccurate personal information
  • Request cessation of use or deletion of personal information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at concierge@kanagawapremier.jp.

2.8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
