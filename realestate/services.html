Our Services

Home › Services

Comprehensive, bilingual real estate services designed for international families, global professionals and overseas investors seeking exceptional homes in Kanagawa.

What We Offer

From your first enquiry to settled living, our advisors guide every stage with clarity, local expertise and an unwavering commitment to your interests.

Rental Brokerage

A curated search for high-quality apartments and houses, including foreign-resident-friendly options. We coordinate viewings, negotiate terms and explain deposits, guarantor requirements and lease documentation in English.

Expats & Families

Property Purchase

Independent guidance for acquiring a primary residence, second home or legacy property in Japan. Our advisors oversee market analysis, due diligence, negotiations, bilingual document review and completion.

Buyers & Investors

Investment Advisory

Research-led advice for international buyers seeking income, capital preservation or long-term exposure to Japanese real estate. We assess location, yield, operating costs, liquidity and exit strategy.

Overseas Investors

Relocation Support

Practical assistance for a seamless arrival, from neighborhood orientation and school guidance to utilities, banking introductions, internet setup and move-in coordination.

New Arrivals

Property Management

Attentive management for overseas and domestic owners, including tenant liaison, rent administration, inspections, maintenance coordination and bilingual reporting.

Property Owners

Contract & Documentation Support

Clear English-language support for applications, leases, purchase agreements and related procedures. Where specialist advice is required, we coordinate with qualified judicial scriveners, tax accountants and immigration professionals.

Expat-Focused Support

We understand the unique challenges international residents face in Japan. These specialized services set us apart.

No-Guarantor Properties

Access to a curated selection of landlords and properties that accept guarantor companies in place of a personal Japanese guarantor — removing one of the most common barriers for foreign residents.

English Contract Support

Full bilingual explanation of lease agreements, purchase contracts and the important matters disclosure. We ensure you understand every clause, cost and obligation before you commit.

Life Setup Assistance

Practical help establishing your life in Japan — bank account introductions, utility registration, internet setup, ward office procedures and school enrollment guidance, all coordinated in English.
